# Modeling Review Checklist

Use only the sections appropriate to the requested scope and level. Missing evidence remains visible, not a reason to invent answers.

- Scope, purpose, as-is/to-be, stakeholders and source evidence are clear.
- Events use past-tense business language, with actual and proposed events distinguished; timeline preserves branches, parallel paths, failures and uncertainty where material.
- Key actors, commands, decisions, read information and external systems are recorded for the chosen process; rejections and hotspots remain visible.
- Terms are defined within their context; candidate contexts have responsibility, ownership, language and relationships supported by evidence.
- Aggregate boundaries reflect real invariants and consistency requirements; entities, values, lifecycle, operations and events are justified rather than inferred from nouns alone.
- Cross-boundary messages, ownership, translation, failure and eventual consistency are considered when applicable.
- Normal, boundary and failure scenarios exercise important rules and observable outcomes; sources and confirmation status are traceable.
- Decisions, disagreements, assumptions, owners and next actions are captured. Mark incomplete work as such and resume from the saved board.
